Measured restore work in three consecutive CI stages of the same Storefront commit:

  Stage "build":    9,800 ms  Restore   1 calls   |  1,200 ms  CoreCompile
  Stage "test":     9,650 ms  Restore   1 calls   |  1,150 ms  CoreCompile
  Stage "publish":  9,720 ms  Restore   1 calls   |  1,180 ms  CoreCompile

The pipeline invokes dotnet build, dotnet test and dotnet publish directly.
There is no separate dotnet restore step and no --no-restore flag.
packages.lock.json, PackageReference items and NuGet.config have not changed
between the stages. The first build supplies the required initial restore.
These timings establish repeated restore work; they do not establish project,
configuration, framework, runtime-identifier or assets-workspace coverage for
a proposed suppression change.
